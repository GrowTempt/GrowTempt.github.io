Featured Work

CS 175 · Project in AI

MCSRAI

A reinforcement learning agent that teaches itself to build a Nether portal in Minecraft. I used PPO inside a MineDojo environment and shaped the rewards in phases to get past two problems that kept breaking it: the agent learning to do nothing to dodge penalties, and the agent exploiting one easy reward instead of finishing the task. Got it acting deliberately over 250k+ training steps.

Python · PPO · Stable-Baselines3 · MineDojo · voxel observations

Hack The Coast 2026

Prince of Peace Dashboard

Prince of Peace, a company that imports and distributes herbal products and teas, was tracking stock and reordering by hand and always reacting after something ran low. With two teammates, I used Claude Code to build a SKU level dashboard that forecasts demand and generates purchase order recommendations on its own, so they could plan ahead instead of scrambling.

About & Tools

I'm a Computer Science and Software Engineering double major at UC Irvine, graduating June 2027. I picked both because I wanted the theory behind how systems work and the practical side of actually shipping them.

Most of my projects start the same way: something is tedious or breaks a lot, and I want to see if I can make it run on its own. That's how the inventory dashboard, the survey tool, and the Minecraft agent all started.

Lately I've spent most of my time with AI tools, figuring out where they genuinely help and where they just look impressive. I treat them as a way to move faster, not a replacement for understanding what I'm building.
